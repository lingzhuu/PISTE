Regional pathway comparison

Load one spatial transcriptomics section, outline a region on the tissue, and PISTE estimates for every gene set whether pathway activity is higher or lower in the region than in the rest of the section.

1

Load a section

Expression must already be normalized (for example SCTransform or log-normalized). Files stay on your computer.

2

Select a region

Draw around spots to add them to the region; everything else is the rest of the section.

No region yet
Load spot coordinates to see the section
Region Rest of the section
3

Choose gene sets

Gene sets with fewer than the minimum number of genes found in the data are skipped.

4

Results

π is the posterior directional probability that pathway activity is higher in the region than in the rest; 0.5 means no difference. A gene set is called higher or lower when the evidence in that direction is at least 0.975.
